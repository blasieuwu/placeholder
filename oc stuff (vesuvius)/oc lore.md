## the lore of Vesuvius (a.k.a., "the rogue template avatar")
somewhere in a roblox game, he was a normal npc. ever since 2021, he lived throughout the game's history, lore, updates, reworks, and redesigns.
"*damn, i might just be here forever...*", he said to himself.
he's seen the same places, for years. he's seen his other fellow npcs come and go. he's seem all of the changes for the time he's been here.
but then, somewhere in the August of 27th, 2038, a new update comes in. as usual, he sleeps, ready for the day he sees the new things. but things take a turn. a DEEP turn.

he wakes up in the middle of nowhere. the neverending void of deformed and broken polygons.
"*where the hell am i?*", he asked.
he's never been to this place. nor has he ever heard of anything about said place. then, somewhere in the distance, he sees another npc, not knowing where and what game this npc came from.

he asks, "*hey, what is this place and how did i get here?*"
"*ah, child. welcome to the glitchbox. to put it simply; you were deleted from the game you originated from.*", the npc replied.
Vesuvius couldn't believe it. there could've been no way he was deleted from the game he lasted years of his life in. he refused to believe that all of this was real and that he was having nightmares.

so he travelled through the glitchbox, finding a way to know the truth, if his imaginations were playing with him and that all of this was a nightmare. but there was no way he could know. to him, the glitchbox looked like nothing but broken and disfigured polygons. so he went back to the same npc and asked "*well, how do i know you're not a dream in my mind?*"
the npc proceeds to open a mysterious mirror that revealed the game Vesuvius was in.
the place he spent his days, the places where he stood and talked to players, and his friends that knew him so much, was replaced. to him, the npc that replaced him felt like a soulless, basic, and unlively person that doesn't give a fuck about the job he plays the roles in.

he felt betrayed. wasted, a piece of crap in the first place, a failure. he thinks he was just a failed attempt that was never noticed, a retarded dumbfuck that was never actually meant for a role but was victimized into one, a crazy asshole that only ever managed to control himself for so long until he couldn't. maybe it was best for him to have disappared from the game in the first place.
but he can't forget his friends. he knows his friends care and miss him VERY deeply. he knows his trusted ones, his family and friends, and his closest friends, ALL care about him.
he wants his revenge. revenge so harsh and unreversable that his creators will regret deleting him from his place.
